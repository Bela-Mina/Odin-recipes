Description:

This burger has 7 simple ingredients. If you try it, you'll love it.

INGREDIENTS:

  • Ground beef
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Bacon slices
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Eggs
  • Hamburger buns

Steps:

  1. Prepare and Shape the Patties: use ground beef with about 20% fat
  2. Heat the Pan or Grill: Warm a cast-iron skillet or grill over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the Patties: PLace the Patties onto the hot surface
  4. Add Cheese and Toast Buns: Place a slice of cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking so it melts
  5. Assemble the Burger: Place the cooked patty on the bottom bun, add any desired toppings, and top with the top bun
