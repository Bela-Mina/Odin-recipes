This Is Pizza

Description:

This pizza has 6 ingredients, and we use fresh ingredients to make it.

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Olive Oil

Steps:

  1. Make The Dough: Mix Flour , water, yeast, salt and little olive oil in large bowl
  2. Shape the Dough: punch down the risen dough to release the air
  3. Add Sauce Toppings: spread a thin layer of pizza sauce or crushed tomatoes over the center of the dough
  4. Bake the Pizza: over high at least 230°C to 250°C for 10-15 minutes
