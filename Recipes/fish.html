Grilled White Fish

Description:

This is a simple and delicious recipe for grilled white fish.

Ingredients:

  • White fish
  • Olive oil
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter

Steps:

  1. Dry the fish: Pat the fish completely dry on both sides with a paper towel so it gets crispy.
  2. Add seasoning: Rub salt and pepper evenly onto both sides of the fish.
  3. Heat the Pan: Warm a heavy skillet over medium-high heat until hot, then add a little oil or butter.
  4. Cook the First side: Place the fish in the hot pan. Do not move it for 2 to 3 minutes so it forms a golden crust and releases naturally.
  5. Flip and finish:Flip the fish over and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until it looks opaque and flakes easily with a fork
